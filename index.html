The Technology

Sediment management that works with the river, not against it.

Reservoirs have relied on the same handful of methods for decades — and each comes with a serious trade-off.

Check dams

Fill up quickly, carry a high failure risk, and interrupt the river's natural biological and sediment corridors.

Hydraulic & mechanical dredging

Costly and energy-intensive, and, in some cases, lowering reservoir level.

Flushing & sluicing

Can lose enormous volumes of water and send a sudden, high-turbidity shock downstream.

Bypass tunnels

Effective where geography allows, but expensive to build and highly site-specific.

Our Approach

Inline, non-blocking, and monitored in real time.

CHATTIGR's technology intercepts sediment inline — upstream of the reservoir or critical infrastructure — using hydrodynamic separation that doesn't block the flow or force a drawdown. Where site conditions allow, it runs on the water's own energy rather than constant pumping. Sensors, AI, and digital twins keep performance visible and let the system adapt as conditions change, from routine flow to storm-driven extremes.
